title: Monetary Policy and the RBI
source: Economic Survey / RBI framework

The Reserve Bank of India conducts monetary policy through the Monetary Policy
Committee (MPC), a six-member body established under the RBI Act, 1934
(amended in 2016). The MPC's primary tool is the repo rate — the rate at which
RBI lends short-term funds to commercial banks. Since 2016, India has followed
a flexible inflation targeting framework, with the MPC mandated to keep CPI
inflation at 4%, within a band of +/- 2%.

Other key tools include the reverse repo rate, Cash Reserve Ratio (CRR),
Statutory Liquidity Ratio (SLR), and open market operations (OMOs). A repo
rate cut is an expansionary move intended to boost borrowing and growth; a
hike is contractionary, intended to curb inflation.
