Question: "Discuss the role of the Finance Commission in Centre-State fiscal
relations in India."

Marking-scheme style pointers for a strong answer:
1. Introduce Article 280 — constitutional basis for the Finance Commission,
   constituted every 5 years by the President.
2. Explain its core functions: distribution of net tax proceeds between Centre
   and States (vertical devolution), and allocation among States (horizontal
   devolution) using criteria like population, income distance, area, and
   demographic performance.
3. Mention recent developments — e.g., the 15th Finance Commission's award
   period and its approach to grants for local bodies, disaster management,
   and sector-specific grants.
4. Critically note tensions — e.g., states' concerns over reduced fiscal
   autonomy, the interplay with GST Council recommendations, and horizontal
   devolution formula debates.
5. Conclude with the Finance Commission's role in cooperative fiscal
   federalism.

Word limit: 250 words. Time: ~15 minutes.
